UESTTS: UNIFIED EMOTION UNDERSTANDING AND SPEECH SYNTHESIS FOR ZERO-SHOT TEXT-TO-SPEECH

Abstract: Zero-shot text-to-speech (TTS) systems use reference speech to reproduce the voice characteristics of unseen speakers. However, the reference speech also conveys emotion, which may influence the generated prosody when its emotion differs from that implied by the target text. Consequently, the synthesized speech may not adequately reflect the intended textual emotion. To mitigate this issue, we propose UESTTS, a unified framework for text emotion understanding and zero-shot speech synthesis. Within a shared language model, UESTTS combines bidirectional text understanding with prefix-masked speech generation, incorporates global emotion, latent text emotion representations, and reference style through hierarchical conditioning, and modulates generation hidden states using FiLM. Joint optimization of emotion understanding and speech generation allows text-derived emotion information to guide synthesis without a separate cascaded emotion analysis model. Experimental results suggest that UESTTS improves text-consistent emotional expression while retaining competitive speech quality, content accuracy, and speaker similarity.
